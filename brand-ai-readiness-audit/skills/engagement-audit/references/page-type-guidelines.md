# Page Type Guidelines

Expectations and relevant checks by page type. Not all checks apply to all page types.

---

## Product Page

**Expected content:** Product name, description, price, availability, specifications, images, CTA (buy/demo/trial).

**Key checks:**
- Structured data: `Product`, `Offer` (C6, C7)
- Price consistency (F3)
- Availability freshness (F4)
- Clear CTA (E2, E8)
- Orientation (E1)

---

## Homepage

**Expected content:** Organization identity, value proposition, navigation to key sections.

**Key checks:**
- Organization identity (F1, F2)
- Navigation clarity (E6)
- Content hierarchy (E7)
- Orientation (E1)
- Trust signals (E13)

---

## About / Company Page

**Expected content:** Organization description, history, team, mission, contact.

**Key checks:**
- Identity consistency (F1)
- Entity distinguishing signals (F2)
- Fact freshness (F4) — leadership, founding year
- Trust signals (E13)

---

## Pricing Page

**Expected content:** Clear pricing for products/services, plan comparison, CTA.

**Key checks:**
- Price consistency across site (F3, C7)
- Price freshness (F4)
- Structured data for offers (C6)
- Clear CTAs (E2, E8)
- Findability from other pages (E5)

---

## Contact Page

**Expected content:** Phone, email, address, form, hours.

**Key checks:**
- Contact info consistency (F3)
- Hours freshness (F4)
- Form accessibility (E10)
- Structured data: LocalBusiness/Organization contactPoint (C6)

---

## Blog / Article Page

**Expected content:** Title, author, date, content, categories.

**Key checks:**
- Structured data: Article/BlogPosting (C6 — lower priority)
- Date freshness for time-sensitive content (F4)
- Dead ends (E9)
- Title/metadata (C11)

---

## Documentation Page

**Expected content:** Technical content, navigation, search, versioning.

**Key checks:**
- Findability (E5)
- Navigation (E6)
- Content hierarchy (E7)
- Raw vs rendered (C4) — docs often use JS rendering

---

## FAQ Page

**Expected content:** Questions and answers.

**Key checks:**
- Structured data: FAQPage (C6)
- Findability (E5)
- Content freshness (F4)

---

## Service / Category Page

**Expected content:** Service description, features, benefits, CTA.

**Key checks:**
- Orientation (E1)
- Content hierarchy (E7)
- Clear next action (E2)
- Landing relevance (E4)
